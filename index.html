The Social Ritual

Blanked isn't just a game; it's a conversation starter disguised as a disaster. While most card games focus on winning through luck, Blanked forces two minds to synchronize. It's a psychological dance where the goal is to find the perfect punchline—the one that makes you both lose it.

Whether you're testing a new friendship or ruining a long-term relationship, Blanked provides the canvas for your darkest, weirdest, and most questionable thoughts to come to light.

How it Plays

Two Minds. One Winner.

01

Private Selection

You and your counterpart are each dealt a hand of the void's most bizarre answers. No peeking. No mercy.

02

The Reveal

A question card is drawn. You both submit your best (or worst) match. Both answers are flipped simultaneously.

03

The Agreement

The core mechanic: you must both agree at the end which answer was the funniest. Your shared verdict determines who walks away with the point.
