01.

Local Pass and Play Mechanics

Experience a seamless offline multiplayer flow. The first player selects their secret response from a hand of bizarre prompts and passes the phone to their partner. This Pass and Play mechanic ensures total privacy for your answers, creating a high-stakes "hidden information" environment without needing two devices.

02.

The Competitive 5-Round Vibe Check

You take turns submitting your most depraved or hilarious answers through five intense rounds of interactive storytelling. This isn’t a never-ending loop; it’s a structured digital social experiment designed to build tension and reveal how deep your shared sense of humor actually goes.

03.

The Shared Verdict & Victory

At the final reveal, you both vote together to determine the funniest answers from the session. This collaborative voting system decides the ultimate winner, crowning the person who best understood the "vibe" and proving whether your psychological compatibility is a perfect match or a total disaster..
